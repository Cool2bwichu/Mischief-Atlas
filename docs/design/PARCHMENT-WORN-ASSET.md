## Final generation prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: original continuous parchment material for an interactive antique ink map, landscape 4:3. Refine the supplied blank parchment while preserving its light warm bone/honey family and restrained faint physical folds. Make it convincingly aged like an old hand-folded enchanted map: irregular cloudy tan and cool-ochre patches THROUGHOUT the center as well as edges, a few small time-worn flecks, fine fibrous surface, gentle local valley/ridge contrast. Three scales of texture: broad nonuniform faded areas, hand-sized irregular age marks, fine grain. Clearly more tactile and varied than the reference, not just more yellow. The entire frame is flat paper photographed straight-on under even light, no surrounding desk, no rolled corners or perspective. Remain pale enough for tiny dark brown map labels; no black stains, burnt borders, holes, strong grid of creases, perfectly circular stains, spotlight gradients, ink marks, writing, symbols, pictures, watermark. This is the material BELOW map ink, not an artwork of a map.
